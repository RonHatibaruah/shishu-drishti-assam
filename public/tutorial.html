What ShishuDrishti Is & The Problem It Solves

In Assam, 9.5 lakh births occur annually. Congenital anomalies account for 8 to 15 percent of neonatal deaths. In remote tea garden bastis and riverine sandbars (char areas), physical deformities like clubfoot and cleft lip are identified late (at 6 to 24 months), while internal defects like Critical Congenital Heart Disease (CCHD) remain completely undetected until sudden collapse.

Current Manual Workflow (The Gap):
  • Hurried visual checks with zero photo-documentation.
  • Paper referral slips with greater than 60 percent loss-to-follow-up.
  • Median intervention age of 135 days (requiring complex bone surgeries).
With ShishuDrishti (The Transformation):
  • 100 percent offline smartphone edge AI screening on birth photos.
  • Automated pre-booked DEIC appointment and 102 transport.
  • Median intervention age reduced to smaller than 21 days (Ponseti casting cure).
