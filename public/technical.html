⚙️ 1. The Technology Stack (Explained for Non-Engineers)

Deploying software in Assam's remote tea gardens and flood-prone riverine char sandbars means designing for zero internet connectivity and strict legal privacy:

1. 100% Offline Edge AI (Smartphone)

Tech: TensorFlow Lite (INT8 quantized), MobileNetV4, YOLOv8-nano.

In plain words: The artificial intelligence has been shrunk down to a 14.2 megabyte file that sits right inside an ordinary ₹6,000 phone. When an ASHA worker snaps a photo of a newborn, the phone thinks on its own in 1.2 seconds without needing cellular data or Wi-Fi.

2. Encrypted Offline Vault

Tech: IndexedDB (Dexie.js) with AES-256 field encryption.

In plain words: If a health worker is screening babies on an isolated island in the Brahmaputra with zero cellular bars, reports and photos are locked inside an encrypted vault on her phone. As soon as she reaches cellular signal, the app uploads them automatically.

3. Assam State Data Centre (ASDC)

Tech: Python FastAPI microservices, PostgreSQL 16 + PostGIS, Docker/Kubernetes.

In plain words: All data is permanently stored inside the Government of Assam's secure cloud facility in Dispur, Guwahati. Zero patient data is ever transferred to foreign cloud companies.

4. National Standards & Privacy Guardrails

Tech: ABDM HL7 FHIR R4, ABHA tokenization, DPDP Act 2023 compliance.

In plain words: Facial features of babies are automatically masked on-device to protect their privacy, medical records follow the national ABDM health format, and every doctor review is stamped into an unhackable audit ledger.

🩺 2. Pediatric Medical Knowledge (Why Timing is Everything)

Clubfoot (Talipes Equinovarus / CTEV) 98% Non-Surgical Cure

The infant's foot is turned inward and downward. In the first 1 to 21 days of life, maternal hormones keep the baby's ligaments soft. The Ponseti method uses 4 to 6 weekly gentle plaster casts and a minor outpatient tendon snip to permanently straighten the foot. If missed, bones harden by age 1–2, requiring traumatic major surgery.

Cleft Lip and Cleft Palate 100% Free under Snehasparsh

A gap in the lip or roof of the mouth prevents the baby from sucking milk, leading to choking and severe malnutrition. Early identification provides specialized palatal feeding spoons immediately. Corrective surgery follows around 3 months when the baby meets the "Rule of 10s" (10 weeks old, 10 pounds weight, 10g hemoglobin).

Critical Congenital Heart Disease (CCHD) The Silent Fetal Killer

Babies with severe heart defects appear pink and healthy at birth because of a temporary fetal blood vessel (the ductus arteriosus). Once this vessel naturally clamps shut after 24 to 48 hours, blood oxygen crashes and the infant goes into fatal shock. ShishuDrishti standardizes dual-site pulse oximetry (right hand vs. foot) to catch the defect before the duct closes.

Neural Tube Defects (Spina Bifida) Urgent 48-Hour Surgical Window

The spinal column fails to close, leaving the spinal cord exposed in a delicate sac on the baby's back (linked to maternal folic acid deficiency in tea gardens). Within 48 hours, bacteria will enter the spinal fluid causing fatal meningitis unless sterile dressing and emergency neurosurgical closure are performed.

📐 3. Clinical & Mathematical Formulas (Layman Friendly)

Formula 1: CCHD Dual-Site Saturation Gradient

ΔSpO2 = | SpO2 (Right Hand) − SpO2 (Either Foot) |

What this means: The right hand gets blood before the fetal duct; the foot gets blood after the duct. If the difference between them is greater than 3%, or if oxygen in either is smaller than 95%, it mathematically proves that blood is leaking through an abnormal heart defect.

Formula 2: Pirani Clubfoot Severity Score

Total Score = Hindfoot Score (0–3) + Midfoot Score (0–3) ∈ [0, 6]

What this means: Doctors score 6 physical signs of stiffness from 0 (normal) to 1 (severe). A score of 4 to 6 indicates severe clubfoot requiring 5 to 6 weekly plaster casts.

Formula 3: 48-Hour Closed-Loop Referral Window

Δt = T (DEIC Specialist Evaluation) − T (Frontline Birth Detection) ≤ 48 Hours

What this means: The clock starts when an ASHA spots a defect in a village. If 48 hours elapse without a district doctor examining the baby, an automated red alert escalates to the District Health Officer.

📖 4. Key Terms Glossary (A to Z)

ABHA

India's 14-digit digital health ID linking all medical records across hospitals.

ASDC

Assam State Data Centre in Guwahati, storing all patient data securely inside the state.

ASHA & ANM

Frontline female community health workers who visit mothers and newborns at home.

DEIC

District Early Intervention Centre at civil hospitals providing free child therapies and surgeries.

INT8 Quantization

Compressing AI numbers so complex vision models run fast on budget ₹6,000 Android phones.

Snehasparsh

Assam Government fund providing up to ₹10 Lakh for free specialized childhood surgeries.
